A photo scoring at or above the win threshold is a “winner”, eligible for the annual competition. Leave the threshold blank for no winners. (e.g. 11 of 15.)

Presentation

When a presentation mode is on (Settings: Guided presentation, Score Keeper, or Judge scoring), the operator drives the show from the Console with a single Start/Pause/End and one Prev/Next that steps the live photo across screens & categories (in the Active order below). These settings say which screen presents each orientation, which orientation a category shows first, and what happens after the last category. The idle monitor goes black.

Judge mode

When Judge scoring is on (Settings), judges score the live photo from their phones via a Judge QR screen, and the app combines their scores. These rules apply to this session. Judges needed and a score range are required before you can start a judging session.
